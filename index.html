📋 About the Project

Basket of Needs serves as a tool for people who want to track and examine their essential items. We designed the interface to remain simple. You load your list, perform your analysis, and get results without complex setup steps. The tool runs directly in your web browser, which keeps your data local and private.

🚀 Getting Started

This tool supports Windows users who prefer a desktop feel. Follow the steps below to set it up on your computer.

💡 How to Use

Once you open the program, you see a clean interface. To begin your analysis, create a new session by selecting the File menu. You can add items to your basket by typing them into the designated input fields. The system automatically categorizes your needs based on the labels you provide.

You may save your progress at any time. The program stores your information in a local file on your machine. You can open these files later to update your analysis or check previous entries. This setup ensures that you maintain full control over your data, as it never leaves your computer.

🛠 System Requirements

You need a standard Windows PC to run this software. Ensure you have the following:

  • Windows 10 or Windows 11 operating system.
  • At least 100 megabytes of free storage space.
  • A modern web browser if you choose to use the hosted version.
  • Standard mouse and keyboard for data entry.

🔧 Troubleshooting

Sometimes your browser or antivirus program might flag new software. If your computer prevents you from running the file, select "More Info" in the pop-up window and then click "Run Anyway." This ensures the program executes correctly. If the interface does not load, verify that you have a stable internet connection for the hosted version, or ensure you extracted all files from the download folder for the local version.
